# Manotu marketing images: generation brief

Generate each image below with image-gen and save it as **PNG** at exactly the path given
(folder `marketing/images/`). These are background artwork: **no text, letters, numbers, logos or
watermarks inside the images**. Headlines are added later in the design canvas.

Shared style for every image:
- Brand palette: cobalt blue `#0047AB`, pale cobalt `#E3ECFA` / `#8FB0E8`, deep navy `#0B1B3A`, coral accent `#FF6B4A`,
  warm sunlight. Clean, optimistic, modern Malaysian feel.
- Malaysian settings and people of different backgrounds (Malay, Chinese, Indian, East Malaysian), modest everyday
  clothing. No real, identifiable people or celebrities.
- **No brand marks**: no car brand badges, no fuel-station logos, no real app UIs, no trademarked buildings (use
  generic skylines). The phone screen shows only an abstract map with a blue route line.
- Leave calm negative space (top third or one side) for headline text.
