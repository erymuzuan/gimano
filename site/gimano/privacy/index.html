Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026 · Applies to the Gimano apps for Android and iOS and the Gimano service.

In short: your personal places and history stay on your phone. We never store them on our servers. We only receive what you choose to share publicly, plus anonymous speed points while you navigate, which we turn into road traffic within minutes and never keep.
Ringkasnya: tempat dan sejarah peribadi kamu kekal dalam telefon kamu. Kami tidak menyimpannya di pelayan kami.

1. Data that stays on your device only

Home and Work, favourites and their labels, recent destinations, search history, places you add "just for me" and their photos, planned drives and settings are stored only in the app on your device. Gimano's servers do not receive or store them.

2. Backups (your storage, your choice)

You can back up that on-device data to your own Google Drive (a hidden app folder only Gimano can access, scope drive.appdata), your own iCloud private database, or a file you export. Backups go straight from your device to that storage. They never pass through Gimano's servers, and we cannot read them. You can protect a backup with a passphrase that encrypts it on your device (Argon2id + XChaCha20-Poly1305). We never see the passphrase, and if you lose it, the backup cannot be recovered.

Gimano's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gimano accesses only its own backup files in your Drive's app folder, only to save and restore your backups, and transfers that data to no one.

3. Data we do process on our servers

Photos are re-encoded on our server and all metadata, including GPS location, is removed before publishing.

4. What we never do

No advertising, no tracking or analytics SDKs, no selling or sharing of personal data, no building of location history about you. Crash reporting, if offered, is opt-in and contains no location.

5. Your choices and rights

You can delete any on-device data in the app, delete your backups from your own Drive/iCloud, and delete your Gimano account in Settings. That removes every server record linked to you; your public reports and reviews stay on the map but are anonymised. You may also contact us to access or correct data we hold, consistent with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010.

6. Open source

Gimano is MIT-licensed. You can inspect exactly what the apps and server do at github.com/erymuzuan/gimano.

7. Children

Gimano is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page and update the effective date. Contact: privacy@bukitbunga.com.
